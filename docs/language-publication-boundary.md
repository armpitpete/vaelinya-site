# Vaelinya public-language publication boundary

## Purpose

The public website is a derivative teaching surface. It does not create language canon by publication.

## Authority rule

Public wording must be traceable to deliberately approved public material or to a clearly labelled grammatical example. The website must not promote a story phrase, prototype sentence, learner candidate or private implementation into general Vaelinya grammar.

## Current bounded public example

`ena rith vakal` means **I speak truth**.

It may be shown as a grammatical example. The current public source does not establish it as the general Vaelinya greeting.

## Public lexicon

The selected public doorway vocabulary may be published within its approved public scope. Public approval does not imply that every word has the same deeper dictionary/canon status.

## Future grammar publication

A public grammar guide may be expanded only when its source material has been deliberately approved for publication. It should remain smaller than private/live development where that improves clarity or protects unfinished work.

## Drift rules

The public site must fail review if it:
- calls `ena rith vakal` the general/public Vaelinya greeting without new authority;
- claims that publication itself makes a word or construction canonical;
- presents a learner/candidate status as live canon;
- silently widens a bounded construction;
- copies English grammar because it is convenient.

The check is implemented by `scripts/validate-language-public-boundary.mjs`.
